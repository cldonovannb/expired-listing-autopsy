The Expired Listing Autopsy

It finds out why a home did not sell, straight from the MLS listing, then hands the owner a personal letter and an EPIC relaunch plan. Below is the owner's package built from a real Costa Mesa expired, with the address changed for privacy.

Christine Donovan, Broker, Donovan Blatt Realty · DRE #01267479 · 714-319-9751

What the agent gets

A safety check first: the owner must be free to contact. Expired, not withdrawn, not relisted with another agent. If that is not clear, it stops.

Then a report card on seven parts of the listing, each grade with its evidence, and the likely cause of death. This copy's cause: buyers could see the home 127 days out of 441.

What the owner gets

  1. •A personal letter, on letterhead
  2. EEquity Position: the comparison, what is for sale today, three pricing paths
  3. PPresentation Power: the home, described for the relaunch
  4. IImpact: the marketing, with real campaign results
  5. CCampaign Strategy: the first thirty days

Example only. Address, street names and prices are changed or rounded so the real home cannot be identified. The cover photo is a stock image, not the property. Results shown are from one past campaign and are not guaranteed.
